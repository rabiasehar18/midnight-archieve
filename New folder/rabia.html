The Room at 11:11

FILE NO. 011 · READ CAREFULLY · YOUR CHOICES MATTER

Rain tapped against the windows of the old Ravenshade mansion. Elena had not visited the place in seven years. Yet tonight, she had received a letter asking her to return.

The letter contained just one sentence: "Come before the clock strikes eleven. There is something you forgot."

At 11:11 PM, a telephone rang in the empty hallway. Elena picked it up. A whisper came through the receiver.

"If you're hearing this, someone has already entered the house."

On the table beside her lay an old brass key and a photograph with one face scratched out.

THE MIND BEHIND THE ARCHIVE

Every mystery begins with an imagination.

Hello, I'm Rabia Sehar — a university student, creative thinker and the person behind The Midnight Archive.

I've always been drawn to psychological thrillers, unexpected plot twists, mysterious characters and fictional worlds that make people question everything.

I love exploring creative ideas, discovering unusual details and turning little thoughts into something meaningful. Storytelling is one of the ways I express my imagination.

Alongside creativity, I'm also studying computer science and learning how to build digital projects. I wanted to bring these interests together in one place.

This archive is my personal creative corner — a place to experiment, imagine, write and create something of my own.

With imagination, Rabia.
